# Changelog

## v0.10.1 — X/Y positioning

- Free HUD dragging without the former virtual-screen clamp.
- Editable X and Y coordinates, 10-unit nudges and saved position migration.
- Preserved v0.10.0 settings and features.

## v0.10.0 — Frame styles and bars

- FLOAT, SOFT, BOX and ROUND HUD frame modes.
- Milestone bars and visual appearance presets.
- Additional sizing, padding and spacing adjustments.

## v0.9.0 — Interface redesign

- Reorganised STATS, SPELLS and LOOK settings.
- Stat ordering and separator controls.
- Themes that affect the HUD text and temporary stat-gain previews.

## v0.8.0 — Proc tracking

- SPELLS now distinguishes learned abilities from observable meter-labelled procs.
- Named profile slots and additional visual styling.

For detailed development notes, see the historical `YNX/CHANGES-*.txt` files.
