YNX v0.15.3-beta — Colour picker & menu clarity

- Click each custom-palette swatch to open a real in-game HSV colour wheel.
- Drag to choose hue/saturation; adjust brightness; enter precise hex values.
- Apply colour live to the actual HUD and live preview; CANCEL restores the previous colour.
- Works independently of Dragon UI and newer WoW colour-picker APIs.
- Removed redundant border-only Default/Blue/Teal/Violet/etc. options.
- Theme cards apply a complete palette; manual colours can then override its six components.
- Six direct custom colour controls: text, secondary text, background, border, progress bars, gain effects.
- Fixed custom gain colour use by number pulse, halo, flash, and progress-bar gain highlight.
- Prominent MOVE HUD title-bar action, larger reset/close controls.
- Icon-adjacent main and submenu labels with subtle category-specific tinting.
- Added conversion-threshold marker toggles for 100% Crit and Echo, separate from goals and hard caps.
- Existing markers for Haste goals and genuine Armor/Movement Speed caps preserved.

Development beta. Verify in-game before replacing the published version.
