YNX v0.15.2-beta — Cap and goal marker controls

- Rebuilt the two meaningful marker checkboxes as explicitly registered, high-priority click targets with immediate visual confirmation.
- Status text now displays the saved state: GOALS ON/OFF, HARD CAPS ON/OFF.
- Removed the misleading enabled soft-cap checkbox; no verified soft-cap thresholds are currently available from the game's stat API.
- Explained what can be displayed: Haste goal marker, and hard-cap markers for Armor and Movement Speed.
- Toggling supported markers updates the HUD and its live preview on the next frame.
- Preserved all other settings and SavedVariables.

Note: hard-cap markers are not visible in a HUD that tracks only Damage, Haste, Crit, Crit Damage, Luck and Echo. Add Armor or Movement Speed to see them.
