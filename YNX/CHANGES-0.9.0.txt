YNX 0.9.0 - Settings redesign and stat gain preview

NAVIGATION
- Redesigned dark, opaque Settings frame, centered independently of the HUD.
- Primary tabs visually frame their subordinate controls; active tabs use gold highlights.
- Reorganized LOOK into Layout, Themes, Text, Effects.
- Removed duplicate Colors page: accent swatches live with Themes; icon brightness lives with Text.

STAT SELECTION & ORDER
- CORE and ADVANCED now focus solely on choosing stats (no duplicate arrows).
- ORDER is now a single-column numbered tracker with up/down controls.
- Use the dash or vertical-bar buttons to add/remove horizontal or vertical separators AFTER any monitored stat.
- Moved ROW SPACING control to ORDER.
- Selection buttons visually distinguish enabled vs disabled.
- Custom profiles now remember separators as well as selected stats and their order.

THEMES & APPEARANCE
- Skins have live stat samples and theme-specific colours for actual HUD numbers and labels.
- 10 themes: Shadow, Glass, Classic, Bare, Arcane, Ember, Emerald, Frost, Solar, Neon.
- Removed Winged; previous Winged selection migrates to Solar.
- Text and icon size, icon brightness, and panel opacity are together on TEXT.
- Optional border-accent swatches live on THEMES.

EFFECTS
- New optional stat gain preview. Example: 25% +3.4% shown for a configurable time,
  then switches to 28.4%. No guessed values: based on actual stat changes.
- Configurable 1.0-5.0 second preview duration.
- Explains Off, Subtle, Normal with tooltips and readable in-page descriptions.
- Colour flash, heartbeat, milestone, spell indicators have explicit ON/OFF states.
- HUD frame geometry does not scale during the heartbeat animation.

COMPATIBILITY
- Preserves all existing v0.8 settings, spells, proc tracking and saved profiles.
- Lua 5.0-era syntax, no modern 2.0+ frame/mixin APIs.
- Packaged for the Azeroth Survivors addon path as YNX/YNX.toc.
- Automated syntax and simulated interaction tests passed; not yet verified in live game.
