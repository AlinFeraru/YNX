YNX v0.11.1-beta — Stable HUD layout and bar controls

- Auto-width now measures only stable stat values, not the temporary gain-preview text.
  Animation previews no longer resize the HUD or other milestone bars.
- The Damage stat displays 116% rather than +116%; upgrade deltas still show +3%.
- Added bar length (60-100%), fill transition time (0.15-1.50 sec), independent
  animation and gain-highlight switches, and green/cyan/gold gain glow colours.
- Gained progress remains highlighted for the full FX preview duration, fading
  only during the last 0.3 seconds instead of disappearing after 0.55 seconds.
- Existing settings, stats, theme selection and profiles are preserved.
